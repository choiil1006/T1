; LS ELECTRIC XGB XBC-DR32H ladder (XG5000, mnemonic / IL view)
; CPU type in XG5000: XGB-XBCH
; Inputs  (DC24V, 16 pts): P00000~P0000F   Outputs (relay, 16 pts): P00020~P0002F
; Terminal labels on the unit: P20~P2F. COM0 = P20~P23, COM1 = P24~P27, COM2 = P28~P2B, COM3 = P2C~P2F
; Relay rating: 2A per point, 5A per COM
;
; Inputs (from Pico through the opto module)
;   P00000 pad 1 DL (Pico GP12)   P00001 pad 2 UL (GP13)   P00002 pad 3 CN (GP14)
;   P00003 pad 4 UR (GP15)        P00004 pad 5 DR (GP16)
;   P00005 score bit0 (GP17)      P00006 score bit1 (GP18) P00007 score bit2 (GP19)
;   P00008 combo on   (GP20)
; Outputs
;   P00020~P00024 pad 1~5 blue
;   P00025 combo yellow - red channel of all 5 strips (parallel)
;   P00026 combo yellow - green channel of all 5 strips (parallel)
;   P00028~P0002C score lamps 1~5 (level N lights N lamps) - kept on their own COM group,
;                 away from the 5V LED strips on P00020~P00026
;   spare: inputs P00009~P0000F, outputs P00027, P0002D~P0002F

; --- pad blue ---
LOAD  P00000
OUT   P00020
LOAD  P00001
OUT   P00021
LOAD  P00002
OUT   P00022
LOAD  P00003
OUT   P00023
LOAD  P00004
OUT   P00024

; --- combo yellow (red + green on every pad) ---
LOAD  P00008
OUT   P00025
OUT   P00026

; --- score lamps: level = bit0*1 + bit1*2 + bit2*4 (0~5) ---
LOAD  P00005
OR    P00006
OR    P00007
OUT   P00028        ; level >= 1
LOAD  P00006
OR    P00007
OUT   P00029        ; level >= 2
LOAD  P00005
AND   P00006
OR    P00007
OUT   P0002A        ; level >= 3
LOAD  P00007
OUT   P0002B        ; level >= 4
LOAD  P00005
OR    P00006
AND   P00007
OUT   P0002C        ; level >= 5
END
